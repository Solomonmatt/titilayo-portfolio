About Me

Titilayo (‘Yemisi) Jaiyesimi is a results-driven Development Strategist and Client Experience Executive with a strong track record of designing and delivering impactful initiatives that empower communities and advance youth development across Africa. She is known for her ability to translate vision into execution—driving strategies that create measurable, sustainable outcomes.

She currently serves as Customer Experience (CX) and Client Relations Officer at Heirs Holdings and the Tony Elumelu Foundation, where she plays a key role in enhancing stakeholder engagement, optimizing client experience, and supporting the delivery of entrepreneurship programs that empower thousands of young Africans.

Titilayo holds a Certificate in Public Leadership and Policy (CPLP) from the School of Politics, Policy and Governance and is an Accredited International Organizational Management Professional (AIOMP).

She brings a unique blend of policy insight, operational excellence, and people-centered leadership to every engagement.

With a strong foundation in Model United Nations (MUN), she has developed advanced competencies in diplomacy, negotiation, and multilateral engagement. This experience strengthens her ability to navigate complex stakeholder environments, build consensus, and communicate policy positions with clarity and influence.

A passionate advocate for youth, Titilayo is committed to amplifying young people’s contributions to sustainable development. She actively champions innovation, inclusion, and leadership, equipping the next generation with the tools and platforms needed to thrive.

Key Achievements

  • Program & Impact Delivery: Contributed to the successful execution of large-scale entrepreneurship and development initiatives impacting young people across Africa through Tony Elumelu Foundation.
  • Client Experience Excellence: Strengthened stakeholder engagement and improved service delivery by implementing responsive and people-centered client experience practices.
  • Strategic Coordination & Operations: Managed and coordinated high-level programs, events, and logistics with a focus on efficiency, structure, and seamless execution.
  • Diplomacy & Negotiation: Built strong competencies in negotiation, consensus-building, and policy articulation through Model United Nations simulations, applying these skills in professional stakeholder management.
  • Youth Advocacy & Leadership: Actively contributes to conversations on youth empowerment, innovation, and inclusion, serving as a credible voice for emerging leaders.
  • Policy-to-Practice Execution: Bridges the gap between policy design and implementation, ensuring initiatives are practical, impactful, and sustainable.

“Driving sustainable impact by connecting strategy, policy, and people.”
